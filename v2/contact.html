Questions

Before you write to us

Is the audit really free of obligation?

Yes. The audit is a paid piece of work only if you become a client; before that it is the first month of the engagement. If we are not the right fit after the call, we will say so and point you somewhere better.

What should I prepare before the call?

Nothing written. Know your monthly revenue band, your biggest frustration, and whether you own the brand. We ask the rest.

Do you work with agencies or other consultants?

Rarely, and only with a clear line on who owns which number. Our best work happens when one team is accountable for the channel.

Are you affiliated with Amazon?

No. Gate79 is fully independent. We work for you, across the whole channel, with no badges that suggest otherwise.
