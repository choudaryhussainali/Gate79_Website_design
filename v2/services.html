Questions

Before you pick a service

Can we buy one service on its own?

Yes. Advertising only is the most common starting point, followed by listings. The audit will tell you honestly whether one service is enough or whether the channel needs more.

Who actually works on our account?

The senior people you meet in the audit: an account lead, a specialist for each discipline you buy, and one analyst. No handoffs to juniors after the sale.

We already have an agency for part of the channel. Is that a problem?

No. We scope around them and draw one clear line: who owns which number. Most split arrangements settle into one team within two quarters anyway.

How fast can we start?

Within one working day of your enquiry we book a 30-minute call. If the fit is right, the audit starts the week you sign and account access takes an afternoon.

Are you affiliated with Amazon?

No. Gate79 is independent and works for you. We are not Amazon, not endorsed by Amazon, and we do not use any badge that implies it.
