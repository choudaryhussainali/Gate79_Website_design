Services and scope

Each engagement is defined by a written proposal: the services, the targets, the fee and the timetable. Work outside that scope is quoted separately before it starts, so the invoice is never a surprise. We may adjust tactics inside an engagement as the numbers dictate; we do not change targets without your written agreement.

Fees and payment

Fees are fixed monthly amounts as set out in the proposal, exclusive of VAT, invoiced monthly in advance and payable within 14 days. They do not include advertising spend, Amazon charges, stock or third-party production costs, which you pay directly. Late payment beyond 30 days pauses work until the account is current.

Term and notice

Engagements begin with a 90-day initial term, because a full cycle of audit, plan and execution is the shortest honest test. After that the engagement continues monthly, and either party may end it with 30 days written notice. On ending, we hand over accounts, assets and a written summary of open work.

Client responsibilities

You confirm that you own or are licensed to use the products, content and brand assets you give us, and that your products and listings comply with Amazon policies and applicable law. Timely access to accounts, stock information and approvals keeps the plan on schedule, and our own delays are reported just as honestly.

Intellectual property

You own your brand, products and data. On full payment, you own the deliverables we produce for you: copy, creative, campaign structures and reports. We retain the underlying methods, templates and benchmark data that make them, and the right to show anonymised results as case studies unless you ask us not to in writing.

Confidentiality

Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for the engagement. That duty survives the end of the engagement by three years, and covers sales figures, margin data and anything marked confidential.

Performance and honesty

We plan to targets and report against them, but no agency can guarantee rankings, Buy Box share or sales outcomes: those sit with Amazon systems and the market. What we do guarantee is the standard of the work, audited against our own 79-point checklist, and an honest account of what moved and why.

Liability

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss. Our total liability under an engagement is capped at the fees paid in the three months before the claim arose. Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

General

These terms and any dispute are governed by the laws of England and Wales, with the courts of England and Wales having exclusive jurisdiction. If a court strikes any clause, the rest stand. Email hello@gate79.agency with any question about these terms; we answer in plain English.
