The challenge

A good product paying for a bad setup

The storage range reviewed well and repeat rates were strong. None of that mattered on Amazon. The hero ASIN sat on page three for its own category terms because the listing had been written from the packaging, not from search data.

A reseller undercut the Buy Box whenever stock landed, advertising propped everything up at 34% ACoS, and every peak week ended in a stockout. The fix was not more ad spend.

What we did

Five changes, in order

01

Rebuild the listing

Two weeks of search data set the title, bullets and backend terms. The old copy missed the three highest-volume terms entirely.

02

Restage the main image

Flat-pack became a studio shot on a clean ground, with the in-use frame moved to slot two. Click-through rose first.

03

Split the campaigns

Three families, three targets. The hero ASIN stopped subsidising the long tail and bids followed contribution margin.

04

Fix the stock

Reorder points per SKU, reviewed weekly. Peak weeks stopped ending in stockouts and the Buy Box stopped leaking.

05

Close the loopholes

Brand Registry closed the reseller routes splitting the Buy Box. A Vine run lifted reviews on the two newest ASINs.
