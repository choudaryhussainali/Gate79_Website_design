Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy explains what Gate79 collects on this website, why, and what happens to it. It is written for the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Gate79 ("we") is a marketplace growth agency registered in England and Wales, trading from London. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described here. Questions, requests and complaints go to hello@gate79.agency.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details. When you use the contact form, we collect your name, work email, brand name, URL, marketplaces, revenue range, goal and message. We use them to reply and to prepare for the call. The legal basis is legitimate interests, because you asked us to get in touch.
  • Newsletter details. If you give us your email for field notes, we use it to send that one email a month. The legal basis is consent, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Analytics. With your consent, we use privacy-respecting analytics to count page views and referrers. No advertising profiles, no cross-site tracking, no data sold.
  • Essential storage. A small entry in your browser's local storage remembers your cookie choice. It holds no personal data beyond the choice itself.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not share enquiry details with anyone outside the team working on your enquiry, except where the law requires it. We do not send marketing to anyone who has not asked for it.

How long we keep things

Enquiry details are kept for 12 months from our last contact, then deleted. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics records are kept for 14 months, aggregated and anonymised wherever possible.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to our processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@gate79.agency and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted. Access to enquiry inboxes is limited to the senior team, protected by multi-factor authentication, and reviewed twice a year. Client account data gathered during engagements is governed separately by the terms of service.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will date the change at the top of the page and note what moved. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
