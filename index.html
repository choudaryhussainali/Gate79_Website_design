Questions

Before you ask

Who does Gate79 work with?

Brands that own their products and sell them on Amazon, usually in home, beauty, pets, sport or supplements. Most are doing £15k or more a month on the platform and want the channel run properly, not patched.

Is there a minimum revenue to work with you?

We usually start at £15k a month in Amazon revenue. Below that, the maths of a full-channel retainer rarely works, and we would rather tell you that than sell you one.

How long are your contracts?

The initial term is 90 days, because a full cycle of audit, plan and execution is the shortest honest test of an agency. After that it is monthly, with 30 days notice on either side.

What does the growth audit include?

A teardown of your listings, advertising, creative, stock and account health, scored and ranked. You get a written document within 10 working days: what to fix, what it should earn, and what we would do first.

How does reporting work?

One report a month, in plain English. Sales, advertising efficiency and share of search, one page each, plus a running log of every change we made and what it did.

Are you affiliated with Amazon?

No. Gate79 is fully independent. We are not Amazon, we are not endorsed by Amazon, and we hold no badges that suggest otherwise. We work for you, across the whole channel.
