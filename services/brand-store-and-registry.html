What it is

Your listings, your Buy Box, your store

Unregistered brands run Amazon on someone else's terms. Anyone can attach themselves to a listing, split the Buy Box, rewrite copy or attach reviews to a product you no longer control.

Registry changes the ownership; Transparency makes counterfeits uneconomic; and a watch on the hero ASINs catches the rest while it is still a Tuesday task. Then comes the upside: a Store that sells the range instead of one product.

Questions

Before you leave the door open

What does Brand Registry require?

A registered, or in some countries pending, trademark for the brand name, plus account and product information. If your mark is still pending we will tell you what can be done now and what waits for registration.

Is Transparency worth the cost per code?

For any ASIN with resale value, yes. Codes cost pence; a hijacked listing costs weeks of Buy Box, rank and trust. We usually start Transparency on the top sellers and expand by margin.

What happens when a hijacker appears?

The watch flags the seller, we document the infringement, file through the Registry reporting tools, and escalate to seller support with evidence. Most clear in days; the record we keep is what makes any repeat case fast.

Does Brand Registry change the Buy Box?

Indirectly, and importantly: registry opens the tools that close reseller loopholes, plus brand-level reporting. Pricing, fulfilment and seller metrics still decide the Buy Box itself.

How often should a Brand Store change?

Structure rarely; merchandising monthly. New arrivals, seasonal rows and current offers keep a store feeling alive, while the page architecture stays stable enough to analyse honestly.
